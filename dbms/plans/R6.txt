Query ID: R6
Type: READ
Description: Payment history: all payments for a tenant, newest first
Query SQL:
SELECT pay.id,
       pay.month,
       pay.due_date,
       pay.amount,
       pay.status,
       pay.payment_date,
       pay.payment_method,
       pay.transaction_id,
       pay.notes,
       p.name AS property_name,
       pay.room_number
FROM   payments pay
JOIN   properties p ON p.id = pay.property_id
WHERE  pay.tenant_id = 'tenant-uuid-here'
ORDER  BY pay.due_date DESC;
